Status change
The standing "no stage 05 for GRCz12tu" rule was written against the NCBI arm, for a join hazard and a zero mito fraction. Both gates above clear those two conditions for this arm — but the rule should be re-decided explicitly, not assumed lapsed.

Three things that will mislead if read straight off the page

1 · Gene counts are biotype-dependent

Protein-coding is near-flat: 25,432 → 26,698, up 5.0%. The headline 32,520 → 60,968 is a vocabulary change, not new biology.

GRCz12tu collapses non-coding annotation into a single lncRNA catalogue of 27,249 records. Rel-99 has no lncRNA biotype at all — its equivalent classes (lincRNA 1,493 + processed_transcript 1,045 + antisense 672 + sense_intronic 57 + sense_overlapping 7) total 3,274.

Any gene-count comparison across these two references is meaningless unless biotype is held fixed. Both sides measured directly on the two GTFs.

2 · The ID series is disjoint

All 60,968 GRCz12tu gene IDs are the new ENSDARG0016… series. The classic ENSDARG00000… count in that file is exactly 0.

The two numeric ranges do not overlap, so an ID join to rel-99 returns no rows — not fewer rows, none. This fails silently: a merge produces an empty frame, not an error.

A crossmap is required before any ID-keyed comparison. Symbol is the only no-crossmap path, and the ceiling on it is entirely a property of the GRCz12tu side: rel-99 names 100.00% of its 32,520 genes across every biotype, so nothing is lost there. The join is capped at 77.3% of protein-coding and 0% of everything else purely by what the 2025_12 build does and does not name.

3 · MT naming is inconsistent

Only 11 of 37 MT genes carry a gene_name, and the naming mixes conventions: MT-ND1, MT-CO1 alongside bare ATP8, ND3, ND4L.

A mito-fraction filter keyed on an ^MT- symbol prefix would silently capture roughly a third of the mito transcriptome and report a plausible-looking wrong number.

Key mito fraction on seqname == "MT" or on the Mt_ biotype prefix. Never on a symbol match.
