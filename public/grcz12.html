Three things that mislead if read straight

1Gene counts are biotype-dependent. Protein-coding is near-flat — 25,432 → 26,698, up 5.0%. The 32,520 → 60,968 headline is a vocabulary change: GRCz12tu collapses non-coding into one lncRNA catalogue of 27,249, where rel-99's equivalent classes total 3,274. Hold biotype fixed or the comparison means nothing.
2The ID series is disjoint. All 60,968 GRCz12tu IDs are ENSDARG0016…; the classic ENSDARG00000… count is exactly 0. An ID join to rel-99 returns no rows — and fails silently, as an empty frame rather than an error. A crossmap is required. Symbol is the only alternative, and since rel-99 names 100.00% of its genes, that ceiling is entirely the GRCz12tu side's.
3MT naming is inconsistent. Only 11 of 37 MT genes are named, mixing MT-ND1 with bare ATP8, ND3. An ^MT- symbol filter would silently capture a third of the mito transcriptome. Key on seqname == "MT" or the Mt_ biotype prefix.
