zeroshot.bio  ·  a drug screen in zebrafish  ·  a working notebook

What each drug
does to each cell

Every drug in the screen set against the DMSO beside it on its plate: which populations of cells it thins, which it swells, and whether it moves them the way a reference drug does.

Read across a row to see what one drug does to every kind of cell; read down a column to see which drugs move one kind of cell. Three rows stay pinned at the top of the plate: a baseline (DMSO, the vehicle every drug was dissolved in, until you choose another); a reference drug (Sorafenib, until you choose another); and whichever drug you name. Anything you click opens its own page below the plate.
