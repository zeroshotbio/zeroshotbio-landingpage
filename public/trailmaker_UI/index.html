What each drug
does to each cell

Every drug in the screen set against the DMSO beside it on its plate: which populations of cells it thins, which it swells, and whether it moves them the way Sorafenib does.

Read across a row to see what one drug does to every kind of cell; read down a column to see which drugs move one kind of cell. Three rows stay pinned at the top of the plate: DMSO, the vehicle every drug was dissolved in; Sorafenib, the reference; and whichever drug you name. Anything you click opens its own page in the margin.
