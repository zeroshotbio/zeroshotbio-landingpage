HVG selection: does it earn its place?

A note for the team — the question Darien raised, chased to ground across three ground-truth datasets.

the road: ① comp-8 (cosmetic) → ② broad ZSCAPE (looked geometric) → ③ replication (flipped on droplet) → ④ this 2×2 — HVG on/off at each stage, scored end-to-end. The headline.

The question

Our clustering picks the top-2000 highly variable genes (HVG) before grouping cells — first at the coarse pass, again inside each compartment. Darien asked the honest version of the obvious question: is that selection doing biological work, or just making the UMAPs look tidier? Crisp pictures can flatter us without telling us more.

How we tested it

The two stages use HVG independently, so we tested them independently: HVG on/off at the coarse pass × HVG on/off at the local pass = four pipelines, run end-to-end on all three GT datasets (ZSCAPE, ChemFish, DanioCell), scored against held-out labels. A1 = current production recipe (HVG both stages); A4 = all-genes everywhere.

Coarse HVG is skippable.

Dropping it never hurt, and on ZSCAPE it actively helped (+0.137 tissue purity). All-genes carves different but at-least-as-clean broad compartments. The coarse pass doesn't need HVG.

Local HVG splits by technology.

On combinatorial data (ZSCAPE, ChemFish), local all-genes matched or beat HVG. On droplet data (DanioCell), local HVG was load-bearing — dropping it lost ground. Best arm: all-genes everywhere for combinatorial, keep-local-HVG for droplet. Likely because droplet is sparser per cell, so it still benefits from HVG's denoising — stated as hypothesis, not fact.

Crispness isn't recovery.

Even where all-genes won on overall purity, it fragmented the faintest programs — on ChemFish, Blood and Intestine recovered worse. HVG still buys consolidation of weak signals. So Darien was right that HVG's visual crispness is partly cosmetic — but it's not only cosmetic.

the honest gap

Measured on Blood and Intestine — not yet on the endoderm organs

This characterizes HVG's role as measured on Blood and Intestine. Pancreas and liver — our commercially central endoderm targets — fell below the cell-count floor in every dataset, because at 48 hpf they're rare and barely differentiated (the known ~0.56 endoderm ceiling). The verdict is real but incomplete, and any recipe change is gated on closing that gap with an endoderm-focused build.

Bottom line: the current recipe (A1) was best in none of the three datasets. That's a real, GT-backed case for revisiting it — presented for the team to scrutinize, not as a decision.

▼ the road that led here · below the fold

① comp-8 (MiniFin) — cosmetic. The question started on one shared-program compartment: a frozen 1,537-cell ionocyte + goblet pair, already separated. HVG and no-HVG were a near-tie on geometry; HVG held only a modest edge on fine biology. Read: cosmetic — which made us predict distinct-program compartments would be where HVG finally bit.

② broad ZSCAPE — looked geometric. Run across ZSCAPE's buried targets, the prediction failed: no-HVG matched or beat HVG everywhere, and pancreas went from 0.58 (HVG) to 0.99 (all-genes). The discriminating markers were already in the HVG set, so this looked like a geometric effect — the majority tissue dominating the variance-ranked axes and absorbing the tiny target — not feature selection.

③ replication — flipped on droplet. Replicating on ChemFish and DanioCell broke the tidy story: neutral on combinatorial ChemFish, but on droplet DanioCell dropping HVG lost 0.20–0.33 purity on intestine and liver. That flip is what the 2×2 above pinned down as a technology split.

PROVENANCE — three GT datasets: ZSCAPE 813k (48 hpf, combinatorial) / ChemFish 295k (Parse, combinatorial) / DanioCell 55k (droplet 10X). Fresh coarse Leiden 0.1 per arm, local Leiden 0.8 on compartments ≥500 cells, PCA≤50 / 15-NN, banked SEED per dataset, HVG flavor seurat / seurat / seurat_v3, sparse-preserving scaling uniform across arms (A1–A4 deltas apples-to-apples; absolute A1 differs slightly from banked recipe). GT (tissue + fine cell-type) at scoring only, σ ≈ 0.069. Scratch-only run — no canonical / ledger writes.
