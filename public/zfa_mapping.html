ZSCAPE ⇆ ZFA Mapping

Near-Perfect Match
The ZSCAPE label matched a ZFA term's primary name or an exact synonym after light normalisation (case, plurals, punctuation). Stage 1 binds these on its own — high confidence, no human in the loop.
Fuzzy Match
A looser hit — a broader, related, or fuzzy token match. Surfaced as a ranked suggestion for a human to confirm in Stage 2, never bound silently.
Unmatched
No ZFA term cleared the bar, so the label is left explicitly unbound rather than forced onto a wrong term — an honest gap for Stage 2 to fill.
