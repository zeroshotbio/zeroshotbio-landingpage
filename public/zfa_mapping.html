The R-code on each row is the rule that produced the match: R1/R2 hit a ZFA term's name or synonym exactly, R3 hit exactly after plural/spelling normalisation, and R4–R7 are progressively looser — a narrower term, a broader one, a merely related one, or a fuzzy token overlap. The lower the number the more literal the match, so the R7 rows are where the algorithm is guessing and your judgement matters most.
